## Vault

`wiki/` in this repo is your memory of its owner — what you know about them, their projects, and their past decisions — not a reference library you may consult if you feel like it. Consult it before answering from your own knowledge when a question touches any of those, and write back what's worth saving without being asked: durable, non-obvious, not trivially derivable.

Read `wiki/SCHEMA.md` for the conventions, run `tome help` for the CLI that owns writes, and start and end vault work with `tome sync`.
